The Conversation You've Been Avoiding

Scenario-based learning Cognitive-level split Behavioral gap WCAG 2.2 AA 12 screens 15 min

Introduction: NovaBridge, a fictional SaaS company, promoted 14 individual contributors into management in 18 months with no training. Turnover on their teams hit 34%.

Version one was a 45-minute course built around a branching simulation. Pilot feedback was blunt: learners were confused, and the scope didn't fit the runtime. It asked people to apply a framework before establishing why the avoidance happens in the first place.

Solution: I split it into two courses by cognitive level. Course 1 builds understanding; Course 2 teaches the framework and the practice.

Course 1 runs 12 screens in about 15 minutes, with four objectives — each traceable to the screen that teaches it and the question that measures it. Learners meet three managers who avoid the same conversation for different reasons, diagnose which pattern is their own, and see what the silence costs. A four-question check closes it.

Built in vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Claude Code. Audited and remediated against WCAG 2.2 AA.
